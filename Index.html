Tool.Lib — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection

Tool.Lib collects your email address and workshop data for the purpose of cloud synchronization and account management. All tool and cabinet data is stored securely in Firebase.

2. Image Handling

Workshop images and data sheets are converted to compressed formats for efficient storage and are maintained securely within our cloud database.

3. Usage Policy

You agree not to upload illegal or unauthorized content. Tool.Lib is provided 'as-is' for organizational purposes.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time via the Account menu inside the app. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

In compliance with Google Play Developer policies, you may also request permanent account and data deletion via web by contacting us directly at toollib.help@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

5. Third-Party Data Storage

Tool.Lib utilizes Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage) to manage account state and synced assets securely.
